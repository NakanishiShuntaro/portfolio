## Rocket avionics

**PLANET-Q · Kyushu University Space Development Club · Avionics Team Member · April 2023–present**

I designed the club's first distributed **CAN bus network** to improve avionics noise immunity and communication reliability. The work contributed to successful separation mechanism actuation, full vehicle recovery, and complete flight data logging across launches reaching apogees up to **3,700 m**.

I engineered embedded control electronics and valve actuation systems for precise oxidizer flow regulation and emergency depressurization in two static firing tests.

### Team recognition

As a member of PLANET-Q, I received the **Autodesk Award** and **Mitsubishi Heavy Industries Award** (2023, 2024), and the **Photron Award** (2024), for hybrid rocket development at the Noshiro Space Event.
