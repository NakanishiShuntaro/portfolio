## AI for beginner farmers

**Kyushu University, Faculty of Agriculture · Student Researcher / Lead Developer · September 2024–March 2025**
Advisor: Prof. Takashi Okayasu

I developed **Tellmee**, a GPT-4-based agricultural assistant that progressively personalizes strawberry cultivation recommendations as observations accumulate. It integrates IoT sensor telemetry, agronomic knowledge, and growers' multiple-choice responses.

Supported by the **Fukuoka Mitou Program** with **JPY 500,000** in competitive project funding, the project earned **“Yoka Creator” certification** as one of two recognized projects among the 14 projects in the Solve course.

### Turning farm data into practical advice

Tellmee helps growers understand what to do next without having to analyze sensor data themselves. It brings together environmental data visualizations, a weekly farm report, conversational AI, and alerts to present recommended actions and the current farm situation in an accessible form.

### From prototype to production

As a software engineering intern at **SERAKU** (April 2025–March 2026), I designed backend APIs and data pipelines that connect real-time agricultural sensor telemetry with LLM reasoning, enabling production deployment into the commercial **Midori Cloud** platform.
