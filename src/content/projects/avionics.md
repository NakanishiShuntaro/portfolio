## Rocket avionics

As an avionics team member of **PLANET-Q** (contributing to the development of five hybrid rockets), I consolidated three microcontrollers into one, designed the club's first distributed **CAN bus network**, and modularized flight software to enable parallel device development and shorten integration across subsequent launches.

I led the avionics team developing the oxidizer flow-regulation valve control system and embedded safety circuitry used in two ground hot-fire tests and in flight, and authored launch safety documentation approved by faculty reviewers.

### Flight testing

PLANET-Q's official report records a **3,431 m** peak altitude for **Felix Stella** at the August 22, 2025 Noshiro launch experiment. I built and operated flight telemetry verifying barometric separation triggers and providing splashdown GPS coordinates to the recovery team.
