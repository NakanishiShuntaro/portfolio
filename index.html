Senior undergraduate student in Aeronautics and Astronautics at Kyushu University. My research interests center on Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD), turbulence modeling, and data-driven science. Currently conducting research on data-driven nonlinear subgrid-scale (SGS) modeling for Large-Eddy Simulation (LES) using a Tensor Basis Neural Network (TBNN) at Abe-Hisatani Laboratory. I also have hands-on experience in rocketry avionics with PLANET-Q, and in end-to-end development and commercial deployment of agricultural AI systems through the Fukuoka Mitou Program and SERAKU Co., Ltd.
