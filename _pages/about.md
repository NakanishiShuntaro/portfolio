I am an undergraduate researcher at **Kyushu University**, working with **Prof. Kenichi Abe** in the Department of Aeronautics and Astronautics. My research interests focus on large-eddy simulation, data-driven turbulence modeling, and scientific machine learning for computational fluid dynamics.

My current research explores nonlinear subgrid-scale models using [tensor basis neural networks]({{ '/projects/turbulence/' | relative_url }}), with particular attention to their performance in *a posteriori* channel-flow simulations.

My past experience includes developing an [AI assistant for beginner farmers]({{ '/projects/agriculture/' | relative_url }}) through the Fukuoka Mitou Program, software engineering at SERAKU, and [hybrid rocket avionics]({{ '/projects/avionics/' | relative_url }}) with PLANET-Q, Kyushu University's space development club.
